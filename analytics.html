Mathematical Formulation & IEC 61724 Engineering Standards Reference

Capacity Utilization Factor (CUF):
CUF (%) = (E_actual / (P_nominal × T_hours)) × 100
Measures actual electrical energy extracted relative to continuous maximum nameplate rating over period hours.
Performance Ratio (PR):
PR (%) = (Y_final / Y_reference) × 100
Quantifies overall system derating losses (inverter clipping, thermal heating, dust soiling, cable I²R dissipation).
Commercial Tariff Revenue:
Revenue (INR) = Generation (kWh) × PPA Tariff Rate (INR/kWh)
Computed across Power Purchase Agreements ranging between ₹2.85 to ₹3.65 / kWh.
Grid Decarbonization Offset:
CO₂ Avoidance = Generation (MWh) × 0.82 Tonnes/MWh
Adopts the standard Indian Central Electricity Authority (CEA) average grid emission factor.
