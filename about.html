Who We Are

A New Era in Renewable Plant Telemetry & Asset Management

Abhivanta is built to overcome the modern operational challenges of distributed renewable infrastructure. As solar arrays expand into gigawatt-scale deserts and offshore wind corridors, real-time intelligence is vital to preserve grid stability and eliminate generation losses.

Through our central Energy Network Operation Center (ENOC) in Pune, Maharashtra, Abhivanta collects sub-second string-level data across 9 flagship plants, calculating IEC 61724 compliant Performance Ratios (PR), Capacity Utilization Factors (CUF), and automated dispatch schedules.

Our Vision

To accelerate India's 500 GW non-fossil capacity roadmap through intelligent, automated SCADA software and zero-carbon engineering.

Our Mission

To empower renewable operators with sub-second telemetry, predictive maintenance, and seamless reporting that maximizes investor IRR.
