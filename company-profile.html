1. Corporate Identity & Strategic Mission

Founded to support India's national mandate of 500 Gigawatts of renewable energy capacity by 2030, ABHIVANTA bridges the critical gap between physical electrical power engineering and high-frequency digital control-room automation.

ABHIVANTA eliminates operational leakages that erode independent power producer (IPP) returns:

  • Soiling & Dust Losses in Arid Corridors: Overcome via automated waterless robotic cleaning fleets, conserving over 42 million liters of water annually while restoring 18%–24% of lost generation.
  • Sub-Millimeter Cell Degradation & Hotspots: Addressed by Abhivanta AI™ optical computer vision algorithms that detect micro-cracks, diode shunts, and inverter isolation trips in real-time.
  • Intermittent Generation & Grid Curtailment: Stabilized through grid-forming containerized Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) delivering sub-second synthetic inertia and 4-hour peak tariff arbitrage.
  • Regulatory Reporting Overhead: Fully automated one-click PDF generation compliance reporting aligned with CEA, CERC, SERC, and IEC 61724 standards.

2. Six Pillars of Renewable Engineering & Operations

Pillar 01: Turnkey Solar EPC & Grid Tie-In

Full lifecycle engineering, procurement, and construction for gigawatt-scale tracker solar parks. Specializing in bifacial TOPCon/HJT module integration, 33kV/220kV pooling substations, and transmission bay extensions.

Pillar 02: Utility Wind Farm Development

High-velocity wind corridor micrositing, LiDAR aerodynamic mapping, and civil foundation engineering for 3.X MW to 5.X MW class direct-drive turbines with active yaw and vibration telemetry.

Pillar 03: 325 MWh Grid-Forming BESS Integration

Tier-1 containerized Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) energy storage with liquid cooling, providing 4-hour evening peak shaving, frequency regulation, and automated Indian Energy Exchange (IEX) market arbitrage.

Pillar 04: 24/7 Central ENOC SCADA Telemetry

Industrial IEC 61850 & Modbus TCP telemetry architecture connecting edge RTUs, inverters, and weather monitoring stations (WMS) with sub-second alert triage from our central command center in Pune.

Pillar 05: Preventive O&M & Waterless Robotic Cleaning

Comprehensive operations and maintenance, drone infrared thermography surveys, predictive transformer dissolved gas analysis (DGA), and 100% waterless dry cleaning robots maintaining 99.2% plant uptime.

Pillar 06: Agro-PV Dual Land Use & ESG Carbon Credits

Elevated tracker mounting structures enabling agricultural farming underneath solar arrays, combined with verified carbon credit issuance under Gold Standard and Verra methodologies (0.82 t/MWh factor).
