Executive Address

Unlocking the Full Potential of Gigawatt-Scale Clean Power

Dear Partners, Renewable Energy Operators, and Industry Colleagues,

When I founded ABHIVANTA, India was embarking on one of the most ambitious energy transformations in human history: targeting 500 Gigawatts of non-fossil fuel capacity by 2030. While commissioning solar modules and erecting wind turbines is vital, the long-term viability of clean energy hinges on what happens after commissioning.

Having analyzed utility-scale solar and wind operations across the country, I realized that modern renewable assets face significant unseen operational leakages: soiling losses in harsh desert corridors, inverter clipping under fluctuating irradiance, transmission curtailment, and reactive maintenance backlogs that depress plant Performance Ratios by 4% to 8%.

The Three Pillars of Abhivanta's Architecture

  • • String-Level Granular Telemetry: Moving beyond plant-level aggregate meters to diagnose sub-string hotspot degradation in real time.
  • • Battery Storage (BESS) Orchestration: Balancing intermittent wind and solar outputs with grid-forming 4-hour LFP storage systems to maximize merchant tariff capture.
  • • AI-Powered Forecasting & Reporting: Machine learning generation schedules that eliminate DSM grid deviation penalties and automate audit-ready compliance reports.

As the architect of Abhivanta, my goal was to combine computer science rigor with heavy electrical engineering principles. Whether calculating plant-specific Capacity Utilization Factors (CUF), triaging inverter alarms through interactive SCADA dashboards, or modeling dry robotic panel cleaning schedules, every line of code was engineered to reflect authentic control-room operations.

Clean energy is no longer an alternative—it is our primary foundation. Abhivanta represents our technological contribution to ensuring that every kilowatt generated by the sun and wind is measured, protected, and delivered to power a cleaner, self-reliant India.

Abhishek Singh
Founder & Managing Director • Abhivanta Energy Intelligence
