What Abhivanta Does

Empowering Clean Energy Assets with Next-Gen Telemetry & Automation

ABHIVANTA is an integrated clean energy technology company providing turnkey engineering, IoT-driven telemetry, and automated asset intelligence for utility-scale Solar PV, Wind Farms, and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS).

We solve the core challenges faced by renewable independent power producers (IPPs): eliminating generation downtime, preventing clipping losses, orchestrating battery storage dispatch during peak power tariffs, and automating monthly regulatory reporting compliant with CERC/SERC and IEC 61724 standards.
